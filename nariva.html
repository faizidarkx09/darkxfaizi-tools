The real source

Born inside the nut. Bottled outside it.

A coconut spends nine months quietly brewing the most balanced drink on earth — water filtered through husk and shell until it lands perfectly isotonic. We don't improve that recipe. We just refuse to ruin it.

Every Nariva nut is hand-picked at peak tenderness, cracked the same morning, and cold-pressed within hours — no heat pasteurising the life out of it, no concentrate shipped around the world, no sugar hiding in the small print. What reaches your glass is what the palm made: light, faintly sweet, alive with potassium, magnesium, calcium, sodium and manganese. Shake it, chill it, and drink it the way the grove intended.

Single-origin groves

We buy from a small circle of family groves and pay above the mandi rate, so the best nuts never leave the region — they come to us.

48-hour promise

From the morning a nut is cracked to the moment its water is sealed, the clock never passes 48 hours. Fresh is a deadline here, not a slogan.

Label honesty

Ingredients list, in full: coconut water. For our infusions: coconut water and the fruit on the front. If it's not on the front, it's not inside.

How it works

Scroll. Crack. Pour. Sip.

Four hands, three steps, one nut. Scroll through the journey every Nariva bottle takes before breakfast.

Step 01

The pick & the crack

Pickers shimmy up at sunrise and drop only the tender green nuts — the ones whose water is at its sweetest. Each nut is cracked by hand the same morning; if it waits, we don't use it.

Step 02

The cold press

The water is drawn out and cold-pressed under high pressure instead of heat. Pressure keeps the enzymes, aroma and that faint fresh-cut sweetness exactly where the palm left them.

Step 03

The pour & the chill

Sealed, chilled to 4°C and rushed out in small batches. On your side there's only one job left: drink it cold, straight from the fridge, ideally with your feet up.
