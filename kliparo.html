FAQ

Kya free plan mein watermark hota hai?

Ji, free plan ke exports par watermark hota hai. Kisi bhi paid plan par watermark khatam ho jata hai.

"Words from link" ka kya matlab hai?

Jab tum link se video generate karte ho, to us video ke spoken words count hote hain — Plus par 300, Pro par 900 aur Elite par 2700 words monthly.

Kya mujhe editing aani chahiye?

Nahi. Kliparo beginners ke liye bana hai — link do, clips lo, post karo. Transcript editor se chaaho to fine-tuning bhi kar sakte ho.

Kin platforms ke liye export hota hai?

Shorts, Reels aur TikTok ke liye vertical MP4, plus MP3 audio aur Premiere / Final Cut / Resolve ke liye XML.
